# M1 playtest: "One Coast"

**Who:** the user. **When:** (fill in). **Build:** (commit). **Goal (PLAN §11, M1 exit):** a new character goes from Furrowdale through the ten quests to Gullhaven in one evening, and the whole loop (gather, farm, craft a pack, carry it, sail it, sell it) fits in it.

## Before you start
1. `cargo xtask server` in one terminal (leave it running).
2. `cargo xtask publish --prod --clear`, then `cargo xtask world`. Use the prod build so no dev helpers are in reach. `world` must come after `--clear`, or entering is refused.
3. `cargo xtask client --profile tester`. Use a fresh profile name; a profile's digits are dropped from the character's name.
4. Optional company: `cargo xtask bot visitor --secs 7200` circles beside the start and answers chat. For the robbery-at-sea half of the exit, a second client (`--profile raider`) is needed, played by someone else or by you in a second window.

## The evening, as designed
Paper times are from content: run 5.4 m/s, a pack at 60% of that, the skiff at 4 m/s, Hearthgrain ripe in 43 min, labor 5,000 at the start.

| # | Step | Where | Keys | Paper time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | K, pick a skillset. Take Odda Fernwright's quests | Furrowdale Wayshrine | K, F, L | 2 min |
| 2 | Plant the 10 Hearthgrain Seeds (wild ground is fine) | just outside town | I, right-click seed, F | 3 min, then 43 min to ripen |
| 3 | Kill 6 Shore Skitters | beaches either side of town | Tab, 1–8 | 5 min |
| 4 | Gather 32 Saltreed, make 8 twine and 2 Reedcloth at the Joiner's Bench | Saltreed Strand, 500 m north | F | 15 min |
| 5 | Fen Croakers ×8, on to Yeva in Redfen; Hexers ×4 at the tarn (quest level 5) | Croaker Fen, Redfen, Hexer's Tarn | | 25 min |
| 6 | Road to Cairnwick: Boars ×8 on the downs, Wolves ×6 in the wood | Bristle Downs, Greymane Wood, Cairnwick | | 30 min |
| 7 | Harvest the Hearthgrain (20 for the quest) and replant the 10 reward seeds | your field | F | 5 min |
| 8 | Craft a Grain Bale (20 Hearthgrain, 2 Reedcloth), walk it to Redfen Post and sell it; take the skiff | Packwright's Bench → Redfen (1.0 km) | F, X | 10 min |
| 9 | Second bale (more Saltreed and Hearthgrain), skiff to Gullhaven, sell at Gullhaven Post, see Quill Ambry | 3.3 km by sea | right-click skiff, F | 25 min |

About 2½ hours by paper. Experience by paper: about level 5 by the downs and 8–9 at the end, so the Hexers' level 7 is not a wall.

**Watch for:** the second bale. 10 seeds give 30–50 Hearthgrain, so an unlucky harvest leaves under 40 and the second bale waits on the replanted seeds. The skiff has no cargo slots, so the bale rides on your back; 14 minutes at sea is long.
