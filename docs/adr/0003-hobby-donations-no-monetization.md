# 0003: Hobby project, donation-funded, no monetization

- **Status:** Accepted, 2026-10-25 (user)
- **Decision:** Donations pay for the server, backups and the domain, recorded in a public cost ledger. Donations never buy anything in-game. Labor regenerates at the original's subscriber rate for everyone.
- **Consequences:** Hosting must stay cheap, which is part of why we self-host one instance (ADR 0006). Accepting donations raises the IP risk, so content is strictly original and there's a legal review before going public (M5).
