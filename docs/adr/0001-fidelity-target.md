# 0001: Fidelity target is the final Western version

- **Status:** Accepted, 2026-10-25 (user)
- **Context:** ArcheAge changed a lot between 2014 and 2024. "Faithful" needs one reference point.
- **Decision:** Recreate the **systems** of the NA/EU live game as it stood at shutdown (final content patch 25 Apr 2024, servers closed 27 Jun 2024). The world, names, story and art are original. Mechanics and numbers from public sources are fine. Nothing comes from the client, emulators or private servers.
- **Consequences:** Research targets late-era behavior (PLAN §1, RESEARCH §2A). Where the late era is unclear, the spec records its confidence and the choice we made. Scope is very large, so milestones build systems before content.
